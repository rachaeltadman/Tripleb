The Vibes

  • 📚 Host picks three books, everyone votes, most votes wins
  • 📄 400 page max
  • 🎬 Books being made into movies are welcome
  • ✍️ Memoirs welcome; autobiographies not so much
  • 🕰️ No dense old-timey language (sorry, Shakespeare)
  • 🚀 Light space themes okay; heavy sci-fi is a hard no
  • 🍷 Snacks & drinks mandatory
  • 💕 Judgment-free zone, always

Food Heads Up 🍽️

Keep these in mind when bringing food to book club.

  • ⚠️
    Intolerances & allergies: pine nuts, shellfish, dairy (hard cheese & butter okay in small amounts)
  • 🙅
    Preferences: no red meat, no cilantro
